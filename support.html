vouchAssistant

vouchAssistant helps auditors vouch samples of transactions against supporting documents in Excel. It finds and reads documents and compares values in code; the preparer examines every item and signs off each transaction.

For access, roles or errors, contact your firm's vouchAssistant administrator (Firm Settings in the vouchAssistant ribbon tab).

Start with Map Workpaper on the vouchAssistant tab.
